### Project Description
Rec Room is an online cross-platform multiplayer game that blends UGC with interactive social experiences. Players can connect in a wide range of virtual spaces, from hangout rooms to co-op quests. 

## Platforms
Steam, PC Standalone, iOS, Android, Quest VR, Xbox, PS4, PS5, Nintendo Switch
